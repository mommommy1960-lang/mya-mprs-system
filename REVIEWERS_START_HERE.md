# Reviewers Start Here

MPRS is an early rescue-oriented robotics research repository.

## Review focus

Treat all phase, resonance, and field concepts as hypotheses. Review the simulation assumptions, energy budget, interlocks, failure modes, and whether any physical behavior has been independently measured.

## Evidence boundary

No macroscopic wall phasing, teleportation, or propulsion capability is established by this repository. Physical testing requires qualified engineering review, safe low-energy methods, and independent measurement.
